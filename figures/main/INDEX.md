## Shared Figure 1–7 decision rules

The figure sequence maps inherited genetic variation, multicellular disease
remodeling, and their cell and tissue context, culminating in the MASLD Gene
Catalog and then in what models can and cannot learn from it. Every main figure
must also show or link each claim to assay
applicability, biological unit, coverage and donor/source join,
machine-readable evidence state, state reason, unresolved alternative, and
next discriminating experiment. Missing or nonsignificant cells are never
rendered as negative votes by default.
