## 1. Executive Summary Across All Five Dimensions & Readiness Matrix

1. **Scientific & Biological Reasoning**: The core thesis of the Resource—that inherited liver-trait regulatory colocalization (`436` or `429` primary genes) and the cross-sectional MASLD biopsy transcriptome (`8,743` disease-associated and `7,214` stage-associated genes) occupy nearly disjoint gene sets (`26` or `29` shared genes, `OR = 0.80–0.85`)—is robust across cohort leave-one-out, enzyme-trait exclusion, and coding-variant inclusion. By contrast, four cell-intrinsic mechanistic claims built on top of that divergence are biologically confounded: (i) **68.83% (`795/1,155`) of the headline "within-hepatocyte" stage-remodeling genes in Figure 3H**—and **86.79% (`598/689`) of upregulated calls**, including myofibroblast `ACTA2`, endothelial `CLEC4M`/`PTPRB`, cholangiocyte `EPCAM`/`SOX9`/`AKR1B10`, macrophage `CXCL10`, and T-cell `IL32`—have a non-hepatocyte primary lineage (`N01`); (ii) single-sample bulk deconvolution (`InstaPrism`) estimates merged `Hepatocytes + Cholangiocytes` (`hep_chol`) on the same bulk count matrix without target-gene holdout, acting as an endogenous severity collider that removes **86.5%** of bulk stage signal even when cell proportions are constant (`N04`); (iii) candidate Catalog rule `CONF1 C4` promotes **sickle-cell beta-globin `HBB` (`rs334`, intravascular hemolysis raising AST in `MVP_AST_AFR`)** and cytosolic AST enzyme **`GOT1`** into `"genetically_anchored"` MASLD candidates (`N05`); and (iv) Visium residualized `Stromal ECM` Moran's $I$ scores highest in **healthy control `28` (`0.2153` vs `0.0983` disease mean)** in `HMSMA`, tracking normal portal-triad anatomy rather than MASLD remodeling (`N08`).

## 3. Deep Scientific Reasoning & Causal Critique

### 3.1 Identifying Equations, Mass-Action / Kinetic Confounding & Modality Assumptions

1. **Steady-State Bulk Mixture Non-Identifiability & Endogenous Deconvolution Conditioning (`N01`, `N04`)**:
   In bulk liver biopsy $i$, expected count share for gene $g$ is a convex mixture across cell types $k \in \{1,\dots,K\}$:
   $$\mathbb{E}[Y_{ig} \mid S_i] = \sum_{k=1}^K \pi_{ik}(S_i)\,\mu_{ikg}(S_i),$$
   where fibrosis/activity stage $S_i$ alters **both** cellular proportions $\pi_{ik}(S_i)$ (hepatocyte parenchymal loss, ductular cholangiocyte expansion, myofibroblast scar accumulation) and intracellular per-cell transcription $\mu_{ikg}(S_i)$. Estimating $\hat{\pi}_i$ from the **same** matrix $Y_i$ against a static reference profile $R_{kg}$ solves $\hat{\pi}_i \approx \arg\min_\pi \| Y_i - R\pi \|$. Whenever true intracellular hepatocyte remodeling $\Delta \mu_{i,\text{Hep}}(S_i)$ downregulates canonical hepatocyte metabolic genes (`GNMT`, `MAT1A`, `CYP2C19`) and activates ductular/stress genes (`SOX9`, `KRT7`, `AKR1B10`) *inside* hepatocytes, projection onto $R$ shifts $\widehat{\text{ilr}}(\text{hep\_chol})_i$ proportionally to $\Delta \mu_{i,\text{Hep}}(S_i)$. Regressing $Y_{ig}$ on $S_i + \widehat{\text{ilr}}_i$ conditions on a noisy linear combination of the outcome itself, attenuating `92.5%` of purely intracellular remodeling even when $\pi_{ik}$ is constant (`check_n04`). Conversely, attempting to rescue within-hepatocyte DE in snRNA-seq via `c3b_ambient.R` without subtracting all non-parenchymal soup sources mistakes unsubtracted cytoplasmic myofibroblast/endothelial/cholangiocyte RNA (`ACTA2`, `CLEC4M`, `EPCAM`) for hepatocyte transcription (`68.8%` misattribution; `check_n01`).
2. **Concave Transformation vs Linear Mixture Residualization in Spatial Transcriptomics (`N08`)**:
   Visium spot counts $C_{sg}$ follow an additive linear mixture over spot cell abundances $N_{sk}$: $\mathbb{E}[C_{sg}] = \gamma_s \sum_k N_{sk}\mu_{kg}$. Applying the concave transform $Z_{sg} = \log(1 + 10^4 C_{sg}/L_s)$ and regressing $Z_{sg}$ **linearly** on $N_{sk}$ (`04_spatial_projection.py:104, 144`) leaves deterministic Jensen/second-order Taylor curvature residuals $r_s \approx -\frac{1}{2}(\dots) N_{sk}^2$ that inherit the exact spatial autocorrelation of portal-triad anatomy (`Moran's I = 0.925` under zero per-cell remodeling in `check_n08`). Simultaneously, averaging $m$ positively co-expressed Hotspot genes cancels spatially white UMI shot noise by $1/m$, inflating Moran's $I$ by $\frac{m}{1+(m-1)\alpha}$ (`6.71x`) over an independent-gene matched null.
3. **Cross-Donor vs Within-Donor Variance in Multiome Metacells (`N10`)**:
   For $K = 50$-nucleus donor-confined metacells $m$ in donor $d$, metacell RNA $\bar{Y}_{dm} = \mu_d + \bar{\eta}_{dm}$ has variance $\text{Var}(\mu_d) + \sigma^2_{\text{within}}/50$. Permuting nuclei *within* donor $d$ leaves $\mu_d$ invariant (`paired[idx].sum(0) == baseline[idx].sum(0)` at `final_prediction_refits.py:163`). Because ATAC-only Cicero selects distal enhancers co-accessible with the promoter across individuals (driven by cis-genetic variants and donor physiological state $\mu_d$), cross-donor ridge regression achieves identical MSE reduction on within-donor shuffled RNA (`+0.306%` vs `+0.322%`; `check_n10`), proving zero single-nucleus pairing contribution rather than paradoxical pairing robustness.
