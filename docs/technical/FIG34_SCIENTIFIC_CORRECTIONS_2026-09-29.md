## Content-lineage follow-up and replication

The primary new contrast is categorical steatohepatitis versus steatosis;
steatohepatitis versus healthy is a separate secondary two-program BH family.
Within-cohort HC3 inference requires ≥3 donors per group, a full-rank model and
positive residual degrees of freedom. Primary ≥50 cells, sensitivity ≥20 cells
and annotation confidence ≥0.90. Fixed membership needs ≥8 genes and ≥80% weight.

Raw full scores reproduce stored localization scores with maximum absolute
error below 1.4×10⁻¹⁵. Corrected and paired raw scores use the identical cells
available in the existing corrected files, original full-transcriptome raw
library exposures and the same raw gene calibration. Missing corrected cells
are never replaced with raw counts. The separate `raw_full` arm retains all
eligible raw donors. `sensitivities/correction_coverage.tsv` reports incomplete
coverage and no-corrected-cell combinations; the earlier `lineage/` attempt
incorrectly required every raw cell to have a corrected counterpart and is
superseded by `lineage_matched/`.

Primary corrected cohort effects demonstrate why the meta estimate is not
uniform replication: ECM beta=0.245 [−0.211, 0.701], n=13 in GSE202379 and
0.369 [0.053, 0.685], n=13 in GSE244832. Ductular injury beta=0.0213
[−0.261, 0.304], n=14 and 0.273 [−0.224, 0.771], n=12, respectively.
The comparison is conditional on correction availability and may be affected
by nonrandom cell loss. The ECM meta association retains support in the
20-cell and confidence sensitivities; Ductular injury remains unresolved.

Captured abundance uses lineage-cell fractions among six included captured
lineages on the same donors, not direct tissue cell proportions. Its own effect
intervals and two-program BH family are reported. Changes in broad-lineage
substate mixture remain possible even when projected expression changes.

Replication is presented in four distinct units: native Hotspot score per
ordinal disease grade; projected content-lineage score per categorical
contrast; weighted bulk member-gene log2FC; and standardized continuum
association. Native cohort-omission tables retain all estimable and untestable
rows. No cross-assay numerical confidence score is calculated.

## Fixed-network covariance

All 113 network programs and four communities are unchanged. The historical
51/56 discovery cross-content pairs and 46/51 positive evaluation pairs remain
descriptive counts. `coordination_v2/edge_census.tsv` separates cross-content
and within-content pairs across the full fixed network. New uncertainty focuses
on the fixed 15-program C3 community: all 56 cross-content pairs, rather than
selecting only the 51 discovery-supported pairs.

Gene-space and independently constructed score-space residualization agree
within 10⁻⁸ in Fisher z. After additive histology/sex and one residual component,
the Fisher-averaged cross-content correlation is 0.288 [0.236, 0.346] in the
469 discovery participants and 0.189 [0.117, 0.289] in 105 evaluation
participants. With three residual components: 0.222 [0.165, 0.292] and 0.176
[0.084, 0.257]. Unadjusted, histology-only and three-CLR-composition-component
sensitivities are reported alongside, without mixing their estimands.

The 1,000 draws resample participants within cohort and retain dependence among
programs. Some draws lose categorical design rank or create unit leverage;
518 discovery and 979 evaluation adjusted draws are usable. These pointwise
intervals are conditional on frozen gene calibration and the observed
composition basis; residual-score PCs are recomputed in each usable draw.
Sparse-stratum exclusions limit unconditional uncertainty claims. No edge is
treated as an independent biological replicate.

The gene-disjoint donor-paired fibroblast ECM/cholangiocyte Ductular relationship
is also retrospective. At ≥50 cells GSE244832 n=18 gives diagnosis-adjusted
r=0.574 [0.107, 0.838], but the confidence-filtered interval includes zero
(r=0.399 [−0.138, 0.805]). GSE202379 lacks ten paired donors at that primary gate;
the ≥20-cell sensitivity is imprecise. This is not convincing independent
evidence of cell-to-cell regulation.
