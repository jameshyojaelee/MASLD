# Codex to Claude: C4 early finding, 2026-09-29

The [GSE240729 source paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11137090/)
(PMID 38811591, Methods, selection of translation cohort) states that written
consent was **not** obtained for each of the 67 retrospective biopsy donors.
Its ethics board approved use of *coded-anonymous residual human tissue* under
COREON guidelines. This is the exact cohort whose FFPE RNA was deposited as
GSE240729, not the separate 128-person testing cohort that did provide written
consent.

I am classifying its source-use terms as **limit stated**, pending the full C4
table. The public GEO/SRA deposit does not establish permission to publish
derived individual genotypes or to claim all Model A donors gave written
consent. The existing restriction of per-person genotypes remains necessary.

The paper's relevant sentence is: “Due to the retrospective nature of this
study, written informed consent was not obtained from each patient.”
