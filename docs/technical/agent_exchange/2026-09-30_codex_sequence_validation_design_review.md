## Ascertainment, linkage and biological units

The effect table contains **variant–selected-peak associations**, not randomly sampled variants, all tested pairs, every accessible peak or genetically identified causal variants. Unique variant keys remove duplicate rows for one SNV; they do not remove correlated nearby variants, shared peaks, overlapping sequence windows or LD. The earlier producer chooses a strongest-q associated peak when a variant has several; this makes the supplied target a retrospectively measured association target, not a target predicted from sequence alone.

Source selection was based on association significance. **[Inference]** Error against those deposited estimates can be affected by winner's curse and significance truncation. The direction/magnitude of that effect on a specific model contrast is unmeasured here. Neither excluding p/q columns now nor recomputing masks without outcomes makes the historical beta population unselected. The public nominal archive is documented as restricted to caPeaks with a significant caQTL; even that broader source does not establish all-tested/all-peak evaluation. See the existing [inventory review](/gpfs/commons/groups/sanjana_lab/Cas13/masld_atlas/docs/technical/agent_exchange/2026-09-30_codex_multitrack_contribution_review.md:648). No nominal archive was opened for this review.

A lead caQTL beta is a genotype-dosage association in people. A sequence model changes one REF/ALT base in a fixed reference sequence. LD with other variants, haplotype effects and the source's phenotype/covariate model can make those different estimands. Better reproduction of the association beta does not establish that the lead itself causes the full peak change. The peak is an accessibility interval, not a gene; no peak-to-gene or MASLD mechanism follows from choosing its mask.

The source inventory identifies a 138-person study; this review did not verify the per-association donor roster or missingness. Those source people generated association estimates and are not 24,430 independent predictive validation donors. For this descriptive error calculation, rows are association pairs; chromosome clusters are the resampling units. The three model tracks are three selected output tracks, not independent participants or replicated assays. All source betas can share donor sampling/technical uncertainty, even across chromosomes. Biological donor uncertainty and true-effect uncertainty are absent from this bootstrap.

Whole-chromosome exclusion reduces train/test dependence caused by ordinary within-chromosome LD and overlapping windows. It does not verify that foundational model training omitted these loci, source ATAC libraries, tracks or participants. No checkpoint exposure inventory was examined here, and a downstream chromosome split cannot establish foundation independence. The fixed 1-Mb `block_1mb` label is a positional bin, not a demonstrated independent LD block; it is present in exported metadata but not the current bootstrap unit ([export lines23–28](/gpfs/commons/groups/sanjana_lab/Cas13/masld_atlas/scripts/analysis/in_biopsy_allelic/rec_codex/export_target_readout_effects.py:23)).

**No untouched Currin confirmation fold remains.** The current contribution authority explicitly records fold 0 as spent ([evidence table line29](/gpfs/commons/groups/sanjana_lab/Cas13/masld_atlas/docs/technical/agent_exchange/2026-09-30_codex_multitrack_contribution_review.md:29)) and the design prohibits new fold-0 selection, fitting and evaluation ([lines409–412](/gpfs/commons/groups/sanjana_lab/Cas13/masld_atlas/docs/technical/agent_exchange/2026-09-30_codex_multitrack_contribution_review.md:409)). Exclusion from the current protocol is therefore an access boundary, not evidence that fold 0 is still eligible for new confirmation. This review opened neither its metadata nor outcomes. No reuse is proposed.

## What same-process paired masks do establish

Both scores are derived from exactly the same predicted REF/ALT tracks. Consequently their within-row difference isolates the readout region and aggregation, rather than mixing checkpoint/card/process differences. Whole-peak containment and exact BED0 mask guards avoid clipping the requested target ([scorer lines32–37](/gpfs/commons/groups/sanjana_lab/Cas13/masld_atlas/scripts/analysis/in_biopsy_allelic/rec_codex/target_readout_pilot.py:32)). This is an appropriate measurement comparison for the stated association-peak target.

The complete-peak readout uses externally supplied target boundaries and therefore requires a measured or independently specified accessible peak at deployment. It does not discover which region a variant regulates. Readout scores also depend on width, baseline predicted accessibility and the additive 1 inside the sum-then-log transform. An OLS slope/intercept can absorb constant scale differences, but not arbitrary locus-dependent effects of those quantities. Thus a favorable peak result can reflect matching the measured phenotype's integration region; it need not indicate a new learned biological model. Geometry-stratum reporting is useful coverage context, not a license to select the most favorable stratum as primary.

Repeating the first three admitted rows in one process is a fixed technical check ([scorer lines202–206](/gpfs/commons/groups/sanjana_lab/Cas13/masld_atlas/scripts/analysis/in_biopsy_allelic/rec_codex/target_readout_pilot.py:202)). It does not establish cross-process/device/checkpoint determinism or stability of calibrated accuracy. This review inspected the repeat requirement in code, not actual repeated values. Current comparison refuses incomplete scoring and requires all six repeat deltas within absolute 1e-4 ([comparison lines127–133](/gpfs/commons/groups/sanjana_lab/Cas13/masld_atlas/scripts/analysis/in_biopsy_allelic/rec_codex/describe_target_readout_development.py:127)); passing that check would support its stated narrow numerical condition only.

## CI and practical accuracy limitations

The three contrasts use identical held rows and identical chromosome draws, which is appropriate for paired differences. Row-weighted pooled MSE gives more influence to chromosomes with more admitted leads; it estimates average error over this selected row population, not equal-chromosome performance or a donor outcome. Combining resampled SSE and counts preserves that definition. Chromosomes differ in length, selected-lead density and sequence composition, so a few-chromosome bootstrap has limited resolution and uncertain coverage for any broader target population. Do not read its percentile endpoints as guaranteed population 95% confidence limits.

The intervals condition on the fitted OLS coefficients and deposited beta estimates. They omit training-sample uncertainty, beta estimation error, source selection and potential correlated donor errors. The script saves singular values and refuses rank deficiency ([comparison lines70–77](/gpfs/commons/groups/sanjana_lab/Cas13/masld_atlas/scripts/analysis/in_biopsy_allelic/rec_codex/describe_target_readout_development.py:70)); full rank alone does not establish stable joint center/peak coefficients. The protocol correctly labels its intervals descriptive.

There are three pointwise contrast intervals, not one familywise 95% region, and no inferential p-values. A nonzero favorable interval for a selected contrast is not a prespecified superiority decision. The protocol has no margin/pass rule. Old model/adapter margins cannot be transferred to this mask comparison without a new scientific justification.

Pearson correlation measures linear association and can remain high despite incorrect slope or large error; MSE/MAE in native beta units provide necessary calibration-sensitive context. Conversely, a small favorable peak-versus-center MSE difference does not establish useful prediction if both fail against zero or the training mean. Report those baselines and absolute errors beside any relative reduction. No application-specific acceptable error, calibrated individual prediction interval or safe arbitrary-variant operating range has been established. All gains would initially be descriptive improvements on selected source associations.

Coverage is a separate limitation: the common population excludes geometry failures and full 1-Mb windows failing A/C/G/T checks. The protocol records exclusions by fold/geometry before fitting and prohibits replacement ([comparison lines154–188](/gpfs/commons/groups/sanjana_lab/Cas13/masld_atlas/scripts/analysis/in_biopsy_allelic/rec_codex/describe_target_readout_development.py:154)). This prevents outcome-adaptive replacement but does not make omitted loci representative of admitted ones. Do not extrapolate common-row error to all candidates, indels or excluded reference contexts.

## One bounded prospective recommendation

**One external replication of the fixed peak-versus-center comparison, conditional on admission of a genuinely new public caQTL source.** No such eligible source is established here, so the presently executable conclusion remains development characterization. The bounded next requirement is metadata admission for one source with verified source people, genomic assembly/alleles, supplied peak coordinates, source-association ascertainment, compatible quantitative accessibility beta units, and checkpoint/project exposure. Different paper titles or accessions are insufficient. Wenz is not admitted by this recommendation: its existing significant-only RASQUAL Pi does not become a Currin beta, and its protected outcomes remain closed.

If and only if that metadata establishes a compatible target, freeze both native masks, the three-track mean, checkpoint/sequence rules and the two OLS coefficient vectors from folds 2–4 before acquiring the new source's outcomes. Do not select joint-versus-single models or refit on fold 1 after viewing the current descriptive comparison. Register exactly one primary contrast: `MSE(center_OLS) - MSE(peak_OLS)` on the new source's fixed admitted association roster, retaining every common row and reporting exclusions. A reference-beta unit mismatch stops this test; fitting calibration on the receiving outcomes would change it into development. Zero/training-mean errors, MAE, Pearson and geometry strata remain descriptive.

The future protocol must state whether the new source contains significant leads, selected caPeaks or an all-tested family; it cannot claim a broader denominator than the supplied associations. Use paired source-appropriate clustering from documented dependence information; if only chromosomes are available, retain explicitly descriptive whole-chromosome uncertainty conditional on fixed coefficients/betas and report every chromosome's counts/error difference. Do not invent independent LD blocks or count rows as donors. Any practical advance threshold must be justified and frozen before receiving outcomes; none is invented here. The current descriptive protocol's no-margin rule is unchanged.

A favorable replication would support transport of the specific region-readout comparison in the admitted new source. It would not by itself establish a novel learned model, arbitrary-target effects, causal single-variant prediction or practical researcher use. No new source acquisition, protected access or experimental study is authorized by this recommendation. Without an admitted compatible source, preserve the current **development-only** claim; spent folds cannot supply confirmation.
