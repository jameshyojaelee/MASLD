## 1. Conditional SAF remodeling

The scientifically distinct question is whether a program changes with a recorded histology aspect conditional on the others, rather than whether it predicts a composite severity label. A fixed model could estimate separate steatosis, activity and fibrosis coefficients for every testable program, with categorical source grades and donor-level uncertainty. It would produce an interpretable conditional-association table, not three invented latent disease axes.

The existing NASH-CRN separability test in GSE267145 failed its participation-ratio criterion (1.42 against 2.0; PAPER). That result must remain. PAPER explicitly distinguishes unused SAF grades in GSE202379 and PXD051911 from that NASH-CRN test. GSE135251 carries NAS only and cannot identify component effects. A new SAF hypothesis must specify the score system and its meaning before grade-level analysis; steatosis is a histological aspect, not measured metabolic function, and SAF activity is not a separately measured inflammatory mechanism.

**Assumptions and failure criteria.** Require authoritative recorded SAF columns, unique donors, sufficient joint-grade support and estimable conditional coefficients. Reject the separable interpretation when grades are too collinear or joint strata too sparse. Do not rescue by relabeling grades, changing component definitions or optimizing a latent decomposition. Prespecify whether categorical contrasts or an ordinal slope is primary. Age/sex/BMI adjustment uses documented source fields only; inferred fibrosis fields in atlas metadata are excluded. Broad lineage scores remain sensitive to substate composition and ambient RNA.

**Comparators.** Composite NAS/recorded fibrosis alone, joint recorded SAF grades, and a composition-adjusted sensitivity on identical donors. Report coefficient uncertainty, not superiority by counts of significant programs. Correct the complete program-by-aspect family with BH; a claim that one aspect is more associated than another requires a direct coefficient contrast with its own prespecified family.

**Validation limit.** GSE202379 is already part of program discovery and follow-up. PXD051911 is already used, has 58 initial liver-proteome participants in the existing contract, and only 26/117 programs met existing proteome coverage. It is a different assay, with no documented matched bulk-RNA cohort. Protein association is orthogonal recurrence in protein units, not validation of an RNA predictor. Exact usable SAF n and cross-source donor disjointness have not been verified here. This track can establish a bounded biological result, but cannot yet fulfill the independently validated new-model objective.

**Next safe action.** Inspect only source metadata and donor-collapse rules for GSE202379 and PXD051911; list recorded component names, score system, initial versus repeated sampling, available covariates and missingness. The next analysis needs a new prespecification, rather than reopening the failed NASH-CRN test.

## 2. Missing-component RNA program prediction

Let the fixed target be S = sum_g w_g z_g in a completely measured RNA reference, with the original canonical program weights and training-fitted gene transformation. For an actual panel mask M, write S = S_observed + S_missing. Learn E[S_missing | X_observed] and a prediction interval, then add the measured contribution unchanged. Full-score correlation alone is inadequate: shared observed genes create accuracy even when the missing component cannot be predicted. The primary receiving endpoint must be error in S_missing, in held participants from an excluded study. Full-score error is a secondary practical metric.

**Feasible development recipe.** A small ridge conditional predictor or Gaussian conditional expectation is sufficient initially; no new dependency or gene/program weight learning is required. Shrinkage is trained using whole-study exclusions; no random split that mixes technical replicates or studies. Training-fitted expression normalization, gene centers and scales are frozen for external use. Refuse unsupported panel masks or contexts. Prediction intervals require a distinct calibration set; nominal conformal coverage does not survive arbitrary study shift by assumption.

**Essential comparisons.** Measured contribution alone (missing contribution zero after training centering), retained-weight renormalization, linear calibration of the observed scalar score, and direct ridge on observed genes. Use matched gene sets with comparable size, weights, abundance and coexpression, alongside the complete unchanged 117-program family. Testing only programs with high full-score correlation or selecting masks after viewing results would invalidate the intended comparison. A model that merely beats zero on missing genes but fails to beat scalar calibration has weak scientific value.

**Novelty limitation.** Single-sample scoring is established by [singscore](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6219008/); restricted-panel scoring using stable reference genes is established by [stingscore](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7641762/); rank-based single-cell scoring robust to dataset composition is established by [UCell](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8271111/). A new MASLD adapter needs a demonstrated measurement benefit beyond these ideas and simple imputation. No first-method claim follows from this bounded search. UCell is designed for single-cell RNA; a bulk RNA conditional predictor is a separate target and must be evaluated in donor-level RNA units rather than presented as a port of its cell model.

**Actual-panel limit.** A CosMx feature mask applied to bulk RNA measures gene omission. It does not reproduce targeted spatial detection, cell segmentation or per-cell sampling noise. Bulk masking cannot make the 105 programs failing current complete-program CosMx observability become observed biological results. Likewise, RNA score reconstruction does not predict protein abundance or accessibility without paired assay-native supervision. Spatial evaluation lacks a full-transcriptome measurement on the same CosMx donors, so a receiving full-score target is absent.

**Stopping criteria.** Before fitting, set a scientifically meaningful missing-component error improvement and interval-width requirement; these have not been selected here. Stop the main-paper model claim if it fails either locked external RNA cohort, fails calibrated interval coverage, or gains come chiefly from programs with negligible missing variance. Maintain refused/untestable rows. A low error in a nearly constant missing component is not useful recovery. Treat per-program calls as a complete BH family and the two aggregate external comparisons as a prespecified Holm family.

**Next safe action.** Compare the actual panel gene axis to frozen program weights without reading expression or outcomes; record missing L1 weight and whether a genuine full-score reference exists. This is an admission exercise, not permission to impute unobserved spatial biology.

### Target, input normalization and strongest comparators

For each admitted program p, define a **new bulk RNA measurement target using the unchanged frozen membership/weights**, not the original single-cell score: `L_ig=log2(1+1e6*C_ig/sum_h C_ih)` on the complete admitted raw-count reference axis, and `Z_ig=(L_ig-mu_g)/sd_g` with mean and SD fitted on the 69 training people only. Set a training-zero SD to one and record such genes. Target `M_ip=sum_(g outside panel) w_pg*Z_ig`. All original weights remain; the missing component is not normalized to sum one. If a program's training M variance is below `1e-8`, report it as having no estimable variation and retain that exclusion in all comparisons. No program may be added because it performs well.

The predictor must use **only** observed panel counts. Set `X_ig=log2(1+1e6*C_ig/sum_(h in panel) C_ih)` for the admitted 714 names, with training-fitted centers/scales. A zero panel sum is an abstention. Using the full-reference count sum as a predictor offset would reveal hidden counts in this masking experiment; it is forbidden. Full-reference totals may construct the held target, but cannot enter features, sample filtering, batch correction or adaptation. No test-cohort centering, target-dependent gene filtering, full-reference PCs, inferred composition, complete-library QC selection or external nuisance refitting is permitted.

This distinction limits utility: an actually targeted assay usually lacks the full-RNA exposure needed to calculate the observed contribution in the reference CPM units. Therefore `observed reference contribution + predicted M` is only an **oracle-exposure computational diagnostic**, not a deployable full score. A practical full-score tool needs an independent documented exposure measurement or a separately justified measurement target and assay adapter. Do not silently use the complete target library to make this limitation disappear.

Prespecify one bounded candidate: reduced-rank ridge regression from all 714 input genes to the eight missing targets, ranks 1/2/4, with rank and penalty chosen exclusively by five-fold inner error. The primary benchmark is unconstrained multivariate ridge on the identical genes and target. Every inner error uses that inner-training partition's target variance and refitted gene/input centers and scales, never the full 69-person or held target variance. The penalty grid for both is `0, 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100, infinity`, multiplied by the largest training Gram eigenvalue; infinity is the training target mean. Resolve equal validation errors toward the larger penalty and smaller rank. Center targets but do not alter their membership weights. Record singular/pseudoinverse handling at penalty zero. The unconstrained ridge is a strong small-sample comparator; added low-rank structure has no novelty by itself.

Primary endpoint is donor-averaged, program-averaged squared error in **M**, divided per program by its fit-set M variance. The single primary contrast is candidate minus unconstrained-ridge error. Additional mean/scalar/extrapolation/gene-imputation baseline errors are descriptive; no second primary superiority claim is introduced by selecting a favorable comparator. Full-score correlation and oracle full-score error are secondary; shared observed genes cannot establish missing-component recovery. Report all eight program errors, no favorable subset. Shared normalization can itself induce covariance among disjoint genes; successful missing-component prediction is measurement recovery, not evidence of biological program regulation.

Use a paired bootstrap of biological donors, keeping each donor's complete program vector together, 2,000 draws, seed `20260930`, for aggregate error differences and 95% intervals. With 20 internal-test donors, interval stability and power are limited. The single prespecified aggregate contrast is primary; eight program-specific nominal tests, if run, form one BH-adjusted family and remain secondary. Training-zero-variance targets are explicitly untestable, not successful.

For uncertainty, split conformal calibration may use the maximum program-standardized absolute residual per calibration donor and the `ceil((20+1)*0.90)=19`th ordered value from 20 calibration donors. This yields a simultaneous prediction rectangle under donor exchangeability with the calibration distribution, not a parameter-confidence interval or a guarantee under study/assay shift. Evaluate its donor-level joint coverage and interval width on the internal test; no coverage statement is valid from the calibration sample itself. Do not retune interval width on either test source.

Stop advancement if the complete-target join or person grouping fails, candidate improvement versus unconstrained ridge has a 95% interval including zero, descriptive errors reveal that a simpler calibration already performs as well, or interval width fails a receiving application's prespecified tolerance. No application tolerance has yet been justified, so a small numerical gain cannot establish practical usefulness. Passing these comparisons would support a computational measurement experiment for eight programs; it would not establish a biologically novel reusable 117-program model. Never recover apparent utility by learning new membership weights, selecting a different mask, adding stage labels or testing closed hypotheses again.
