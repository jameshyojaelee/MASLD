### Realistic use and limit

**[Inference]** RNA alone can supply a fixed prediction of concentration-adjusted
regional deviations in the development target's units. A researcher could
use these predictions to prioritize regions or RNA-defined differences for
subsequent chromatin measurement, with calibrated scope and uncertainty if
the fixed transport test supports them. That use does not require observing
the target at prediction time; validation does require H3 measurements to
construct the same residual endpoint. Predictions are not measured enhancer
activity, a complete chromatin map, total H3 abundance, or guaranteed actual
regional enrichment in a new biopsy. Rankings across regions need particular
care because each region has its own removed concentration contribution.

Even a future unresidualized CPM head would predict a library-relative
distribution, not absolute H3K27ac molecules or mark abundance without an
appropriate physical calibration. Adding an average development concentration
term, estimating descriptors from receiving H3, or fitting a new head would
be a different prediction/measurement contract. None is a pre-existing frozen
RNA-only measured-H3 output here. This review changes neither the current
receiving primary comparison nor its target. Any prospective new development
head requires a separate design before access to receiving accuracy results.
