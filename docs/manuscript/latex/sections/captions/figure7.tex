\textbf{Figure 7. Predictive models are scored against task-specific baselines beside a record of their training exposure.}

\textbf{(A)} Evaluation framework. Each of 8 registered tasks has a baseline set in advance. A score whose exposure record is unresolved is uninterpretable, not evidence of skill. The bar counts 126 audit entries by checkpoint finding, including 37 no-finding placeholders, before eight later audit revisions. These checkpoint findings are separate from the study-specific exposure in (C) and (F).

\textbf{(B)} Gain over each task's own baseline, named with its score on each x axis. Reporter assay (GSE281364, HepG2 contexts only): Bonferroni-corrected intervals across the seven rows (paired block bootstrap). Sequence-based reporter baselines were run: HyenaDNA scores 0.216 against allele-identity ridge (B0) 0.134, CpG ridge (B1) 0.178, composition (B2) 0.176 and the prespecified flank and dinucleotide ridge (B3) 0.167. The gain over B3 is 0.049 (95\% 0.0006 to 0.099; Bonferroni-7 lower bound -0.015) and over B1 0.038 (95\% -0.006 to 0.083), so the corrected gain holds only against allele identity. eQTL \textbar z\textbar{} and sign: held-out LD fold, paired 95\% LD-block bootstrap. Fibrosis: samples are the unit because the training pool has no donor key; 95\% sample bootstrap. H3K27ac: 95\% participant bootstrap. ATAC: donor jackknife.

\textbf{(C)} Donor-class-balanced macro F1 for broad cell classes on all seven held-out studies (hollow; 102 donors, 50,000 cells) and on each encoder's clean studies (filled). Gray: HVG+PCA with the same head. Intervals are 95\% study-stratified donor-cluster bootstraps. Geneformer V2 has no clean study, so its scores are not evidence of skill. Hollow and filled scores use different study sets and are not compared with each other; (F) compares each encoder with HVG+PCA on the same studies.

\textbf{(D)} Signed eQTL: best head's sign AUC per \textbar z\textbar{} stratum (95\% LD-block bootstrap, uncorrected across five strata) beside within-block permutation chance and the sequence-attributable bound, which assumes at most one causal variant per gene and LD block. Severity transfer: released instrument, no refit; Spearman with stage (stage-stratified 95\% bootstrap) beside each cohort's stage tie ceiling. GSE213621 units are genetic identities, not verified donors. GSE276114 includes participants with HCC, sampled from peritumoral liver without a per-sample label. LD-block join: Pearson r of each row's effect with its gene's mean effect, own LD block left in or held out. Routing: fitted rule and oracle router against the pre-declared floor.

\textbf{(E)} Each statistic is oriented so that positive is the prespecified direction and placed on one scale, (estimate − null)/(threshold − null): 0 is the null, 1 the threshold its verdict uses (minimum detectable effect for severity arms and the zone task; h2 z = 7 for GWAS rows; twice the test's resolution for signed eQTL; the pre-declared floor for routing). Severity-arm intervals (95\% stratified bootstrap) show precision only; verdicts use the label permutation null, and an underpowered arm is untestable, not negative. Signed eQTL direction, recorded as tested negative, is drawn untestable because its sequence-attributable bound (open diamond) is below the threshold. Treatment response uses the GSE83452 diet arm (6 resolved vs 16 persisted; permutation p 0.23). Its underpowered result is drawn as untestable rather than a tested negative. Routing is also untestable because its comparison does not establish an adequately powered negative result.

\textbf{(F)} Top: exposure of each held-out study (donors in parentheses) for each encoder. Middle: macro F1 gain over HVG+PCA with the same head, on all seven studies (hollow) and clean studies (filled). Bottom: encoder minus the scVI liver encoder trained here, on the encoder's clean studies, drawn as the donor-pooled estimate. For TranscriptFormer minus scVI (linear head), the donor-pooled difference is +0.0154 and the equal-study mean is -0.0042; both intervals exclude zero, so the sign depends on the estimand. Intervals are 95\% donor-cluster bootstraps, uncorrected across rows. In the unweighted study mean, a study with 2 donors and 149 cells gets one seventh of the weight; two of twelve model-by-head blocks change sign when weighted by donors, so no sign claim rests on the unweighted mean.
