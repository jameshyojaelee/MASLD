# Summary

Gene-level evidence from inherited variation and from the disease-state liver transcriptome of established metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) is rarely recorded side by side with what each assay could test. Here, we assembled a human MASLD Resource combining bulk liver RNA sequencing from five cohorts, analyzed as 774 genetic-identity clusters rather than verified individuals, with liver eQTL colocalization of 35 liver-trait GWAS strata. We identified 1,854 disease-state calls and 436 multi-signal colocalized genes. For 423 of the 436, the strongest colocalization came from a liver-enzyme trait rather than a direct disease or liver-fat trait. Among 3,769 genes evaluated by multi-signal colocalization, we detected neither enrichment nor depletion of genes in both sets within expression deciles, although expression was not fully controlled. Most disease-state calls without colocalization had no multi-signal colocalization result, so they are not genetic negatives. The MASLD Gene Catalog records genetic and disease-state evidence separately for 28,233 gene identities, separates untested genes from genes tested without support, and proposes a next experiment for each gene; for 94.5% of genes, that experiment is to obtain the missing evidence layer.
