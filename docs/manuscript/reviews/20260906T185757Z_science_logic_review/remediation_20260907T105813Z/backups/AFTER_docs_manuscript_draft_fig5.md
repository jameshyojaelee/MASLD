## Assay-native molecular and physical context qualifies Resource nominations

> Working Figure 5 prose aligned to the six adopted panel files.

Figure 5 places inherited genetic signals and established disease programs in
liver protein, accessible chromatin, and intact tissue, each in its own units
(Figure 5A). Model specifications, correction families and evidence-state
definitions are in Methods; panel construction and denominators are in the
legends. Every panel carries its applicability, evidence state, unresolved
alternative, and next discriminating experiment in its source table.

Figure 5B places every measured prioritized gene in the adjusted PXD051911
liver proteome (58 patients). Of the genes reaching protein significance, most
change in the same direction as their transcript; nonsignificance is not
interpreted as falsification.

The protein data complete the GNMT–MAT1A–CYP2C19 example introduced in Figure
3: all three proteins decrease in disease with BH q<0.05, in the same direction
as pooled RNA. This is assay-native support for a severity-linked loss of
hepatocyte metabolic function, not evidence that the three genes share one
inherited regulatory mechanism.

Figure 5C retains a fixed 25-protein process view. Because the same cohort
contributed to row selection, it is descriptive rather than independent
validation.

Figure 5D intersects the regulatory posterior of 816 colocalized gene-study
pairs with open chromatin from two liver snATAC sources across five shared
lineages, keeping direct MASLD/liver-fat and liver-enzyme traits separate. Peak
overlap localizes regulatory DNA; it does not assign a variant to a neighboring
gene, and absent overlap is unresolved context rather than a negative test.

Figure 5F resolves tissue organization for the two prespecified robust
programs. Both ECM/IGFBP7 and ductular-injury/BICC1 are positive by aggregate
spatial autocorrelation, yet only ECM/IGFBP7 exceeds a matched null built from
genes of equivalent expression, detection, biotype and lineage correlation;
ductular-injury/BICC1 is indeterminate rather than negative. Two equally robust
disease programs therefore differ in whether their tissue organization is
program-specific, and the raw statistic does not separate them. Disease
association, cell-of-origin assignment and tissue architecture are separate
claims requiring separate calibration. Donor joins, array-level source
dependence and the matched-null construction are in Methods.
