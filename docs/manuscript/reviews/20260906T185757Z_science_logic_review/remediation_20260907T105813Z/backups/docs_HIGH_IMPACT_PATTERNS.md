# Fifty high-impact patterns in the human MASLD Resource

**Prepared:** 2026-08-13  
**Purpose:** Ranked systems-level findings and bounded follow-up analyses for manuscript emphasis.  
**Release warning:** This document is an analytical synthesis, not a release authority. `docs/PAPER.md`, `docs/STATUS.md`, `docs/RESULTS.md`, and `docs/ROADMAP.md` remain authoritative.

## Recommended paper identity

This is not another MASLD atlas. It is a human MASLD evidence-resolution system showing which biological signals survive observability, provenance, donor-level inference, matched nulls, and molecular-object boundaries.

The flagship biological result is the stage-independent multicellular coordination architecture, especially the ductular–portal fibroblast–cholangiocyte community. The flagship empirical demonstration is spatial calibration separating supported IGFBP7 organization from indeterminate BICC1 organization.

The work is differentiated from resources centered on outcome-linked bulk transcriptomics, harmonized RNA atlases, molecular trajectories, functional risk-allele maps, and spatial MASLD atlases. The combination of seven-state evidence rules, source-aware testability, matched-null calibration, and molecular-object-specific experiment routing is the defensible novelty.

## Shared inference rules

- Bulk stage models use participants with fixed cohort and inferred sex, with BH over all 23,370 genes per contrast.
- Single-cell inference collapses measurements to biological donors.
- ATAC uses donors with BH correction within lineage-by-cohort families.
- Spatial confirmation uses donor-first estimates and 9,999 matched nulls, with BH over two prespecified programs.
- Protein models use 58 participants with batch, age, BMI, and sex adjustment.

- ~~The trait-directness bootstrap needs multiplicity correction and must call its 1-Mb groups operational clusters, not independent loci.~~ **RESOLVED 2026-08-17** (`GWAS/finemapping/results/fig2_multiplicity/20260817T161128Z/`). BH over the six-test family; four survive (coding q=6.0e-4, unresolved-context q=6.0e-4, promoter q=0.0042, resolved-no-context q=0.0085), two do not (accessibility q=0.222, **ABC q=0.398, retired**). Two consequences the correction exposed. (i) The published p is a *bootstrap* p, `2*min((diff<=0).mean(),(diff>=0).mean())`, which asks whether a difference clears zero, not whether the trait-class grouping produces it; the two nulls disagree in **both** directions across this family, so both are now reported per test and BH is applied to the permutation p. (ii) A permutation statistic of `|log(ratio of means)|` is invalid on these zero-inflated masses — 74.1% of enzyme clusters have zero coding mass, so a random subset falls 7× below the pooled mean easily and never rises 7× above it; use the difference of fractions.
- Figure 4D's communication source does not expose the full FDR family that generated q=0.0692.
