# MASLD / MASH Therapeutics Pipeline

This document catalogs the current therapeutics for Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatohepatitis (MASH) and Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease (MASLD), including both FDA-approved therapies and pipeline drugs in clinical trials (Phase 2 and Phase 3). The list is sorted alphabetically by company name.

## Fact-Check of Provided Data
*   **Rezdiffra (Resmetirom):** **True**. FDA-approved in March 2024 for MASH with moderate-to-advanced fibrosis. It is a THR-β agonist.
*   **Semaglutide:** **True**. FDA-approved for MASH in August 2025 based on the Phase 3 ESSENCE trial. It is a GLP-1 receptor agonist.
*   **FGF21 Analogs:** **True**. Drugs like Efruxifermin and Pegozafermin are indeed in Phase 3 trials.
*   **Survodutide:** **True**. Currently in Phase 3 trials (including for patients with cirrhosis) and acts as a GLP-1/Glucagon dual agonist.
*   **Lanifibranor:** **True**. Currently in Phase 3 (the NATiV3 trial) and is a Pan-PPAR agonist.
